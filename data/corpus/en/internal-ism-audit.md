# Internal ISM Audit

**Document:** SMS-EN-02 · **Revision:** 5 · **Date:** 2026-03-14 · **Fleet:** Chartroom Demo Fleet

> Synthetic document for the Chartroom demo. Not an operative procedure and not a substitute for a company SMS.

## 1. Purpose
Internal audits verify that the safety management system works on board as described and show where it needs to improve.

## 2. Scope and frequency
Every vessel of the demo fleet is audited at least once in every 12-month period. Additional audits are ordered after a detention, a serious accident or a major non-conformity.

## 3. Auditors
Audits are carried out by trained auditors who are independent of the area being audited. An officer serving on a vessel cannot audit that vessel.

## 4. Audit process
### 4.1 Planning
The vessel is notified at least two weeks before the audit. The notice includes the scope, the dates and the list of records to prepare.

### 4.2 On-board audit
The audit opens with a meeting with the Master and senior officers. The auditor interviews crew members, observes work in progress and samples records such as drill logs, permits and maintenance reports.

### 4.3 Classification of findings
- Major non-conformity: a deviation that poses a serious threat to people, the ship or the environment, or a failure to implement a requirement of the ISM Code.
- Non-conformity: a specified requirement that is not fulfilled.
- Observation: a fact that could become a non-conformity if not addressed.

## 5. Follow-up
A corrective action plan is agreed within 30 days of the audit. A major non-conformity requires immediate action and is reported to the DPA within 24 hours. The DPA verifies that each corrective action is effective before closing the finding.

## 6. Records
Audit reports and corrective action records are kept for at least three years.
