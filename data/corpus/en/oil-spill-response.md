# Oil Spill Response on Board

**Document:** SMS-EN-12 · **Revision:** 1 · **Date:** 2026-04-02 · **Fleet:** Chartroom Demo Fleet

> Synthetic document for the Chartroom demo. Not an operative procedure and not a substitute for a company SMS.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure sets out the immediate actions after an oil spill on deck or overboard and the reporting duties that follow.

## 2. Responsibilities
- The Master reports the incident to the coastal state and to the company.
- The Chief Officer leads the deck response team.
- The Chief Engineer stops all transfers and isolates the source.

## 3. Immediate actions
### 3.1 Stop the source
Stop all oil transfer operations, close the valves and isolate the leaking line. Sound the alarm and inform the bridge.

### 3.2 Contain the spill
Use absorbent materials from the SOPEP locker to keep the oil on deck. Check that the scuppers remain plugged. Never wash oil overboard and never use dispersants without approval from the coastal state.

## 4. Reporting
The Master notifies the nearest coastal state authority without delay, using the contacts listed in the ship's SOPEP. The company's Designated Person Ashore (DPA) is informed at the same time. The initial report is followed by a full report on form SMS-F-20 within 24 hours.

## 5. Clean-up and waste
Collected oil and contaminated absorbents are stored in sealed, labelled drums and landed to a shore reception facility against a receipt. The receipt is kept with the Oil Record Book.

## 6. Drills
Oil spill response drills are held at least every three months and recorded in the drill log.
