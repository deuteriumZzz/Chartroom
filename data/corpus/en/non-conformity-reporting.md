# Reporting Non-conformities, Accidents and Near Misses

**Document:** SMS-EN-05 · **Revision:** 3 · **Date:** 2026-02-20 · **Fleet:** Chartroom Demo Fleet

> Synthetic document for the Chartroom demo. Not an operative procedure and not a substitute for a company SMS.

## 1. Purpose
This procedure ensures that non-conformities, accidents and near misses are reported, investigated and used to prevent recurrence.

## 2. Definitions
- Non-conformity: a situation where objective evidence shows that a specified requirement is not fulfilled.
- Accident: an event that results in injury, damage or pollution.
- Near miss: an event that could have resulted in an accident but did not.

## 3. Reporting
### 3.1 Who reports
Any crew member who observes a non-conformity, accident or near miss reports it. Reports are made without fear of blame: the purpose is to learn, not to punish.

### 3.2 How and when
The report is made on form SMS-F-08 and handed to the Master within 24 hours. The Master reviews it, adds the immediate actions taken and sends it to the company within 72 hours. Accidents involving injury or pollution are reported to the company immediately by phone.

## 4. Investigation
The Master or a nominated officer identifies the root cause and proposes corrective and preventive actions. Serious cases are investigated by the company safety department.

## 5. Closure and lessons learned
The DPA verifies that the actions are effective and closes the report. Lessons learned are shared with the whole fleet in a quarterly safety bulletin.
